The hypothesis

A book does not transfer and convert at the same time

Custody moves in months. The mandate moves in years. Everything we do is about closing that gap.
Phase 1
The custody arrives
The relationship transfers with the RM. Assets sit where they always sat — we hold them, we don't yet manage them.
~1 in 30of new money on platform
Phase 2
The trust is earned
First mandate — almost always the core. Performance shows up on a statement, and the domain expert has met the client.
~1 in 7of new money on platform
Phase 3
Platform becomes the default
The RM stops selling the platform and starts starting there. Satellites and focused products get added on top.
~1 in 3of new money on platform
“
Time, not effort, is what converts a book — so every month we take out of the ramp is AUM pulled forward.
Shorten Phase 1
Put the domain expert in the first 90 days, not the ninth month. Make one core mandate the standard opening ask.
Compress Phase 2
Performance is already the argument — every strategy is ahead of benchmark. Get the statement in front of the client sooner.
Reach Phase 3 earlier
Mature desks show it is reachable. The question is whether it takes 18 months or 9.
+₹257 Cr
What the ramp is worth: lifting penetration from 13.2% to 20% on today's ₹3,774 Cr book takes platform AUM to ₹755 Cr — without a single new client or a rupee of new money.

Strategic questions · to brainstorm

Four questions for the room

One ladder: get the assets across faster, convert more of them, earn more per rupee — and decide where we place the next bet.
1Assets arrive
2Assets convert
3Revenue earned
4Where we bet next
Base camp
Q1 · TRANSFER SPEED

How do we get assets across faster?

The book grew ₹179 Cr across our top 5 desks in five months, but custody still arrives months before any mandate does. Speed of transfer is the cheapest AUM we can buy.
To decideWhere do we actually lose the time — documentation, custody operations, or the client’s own inertia?
The climb
Q2 · PENETRATION

How do we lift platform penetration?

Only 13.2% of a ₹3,774 Cr book is on platform. Twelve large books sit under 10% — ₹1,349 Cr already in our custody and in play.
To decideDo we set a desk-level penetration floor — and what support ships with it?
High camp
Q3 · REVENUE

How do we turn platform AUM into revenue?

Focused products are ₹7 Cr on a ₹498 Cr platform. Same client, same trust — the next rung is yield per client, not more clients.
To decideWhich single product do we put in front of every platform client this quarter?
The summit
Q4 · WHERE WE BET

Deepen the desks we have, or widen the shelf?

Top 5 RMs hold 26% of platform AUM; the global shelf is only now going live. Do we back the 72 desks we have, or the products they could be selling?
To decideDo the next twelve months go into RM capacity, or into the product shelf?
One thread — move transferred custody into platform and focused products, faster. That is where performance and margin compound.
