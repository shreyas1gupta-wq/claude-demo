# Completeness Review — NDPMS ₹500 Cr Blueprint (v1)

Reviewer: completeness critic. Compared `blueprint_v1.md` + `blueprint_data.json` against all seven `design_*` notes, both `data_*` packs, and all four `critique_*` reviews in the same folder.

## Verdict

The blueprint is a genuine, well-executed synthesis: nearly every HIGH finding from all four critiques (110%-summing sleeve table, third-party PMS booked inside AUM, the sel/tco.py 100x tax-drag bug, the Reg 22/31 citation split, the ₹88 stale USDINR, the four competing Policy Composites, the credit-floor breach, the "must"-labelled self-executing de-risk trigger, missing AML/KYC/PMLA, missing AIF capital-call standing instruction, thin team sizing) is explicitly corrected and logged in §13. Sections 0–13 and both appendices exist with real, load-bearing content, not placeholders, and every allocation table in the narrative sums to 100%/₹500 Cr. However, the merge was not fully propagated into `blueprint_data.json`: several of the narrative's own corrections (AA-credit trim to 2%, TMF raised to 13%, the 3%/5% single-stock cap, the "not subtracted twice" implementation-shortfall rule) are contradicted by stale or differently-computed figures still sitting in the structured pack, and the JSON's deployment plan describes a different ladder than the narrative's. A handful of critic-flagged items (succession/incapacity beyond "unreachable," Angel-One best-execution on group trades, capture-ratio backtesting, a costed FX hedge, business-income-heavy Cat III scenario, NRI/FEMA branch logic) remain open or only namechecked. None of this undermines the overall architecture, but the JSON pack needs a reconciliation pass before it is treated as machine-readable truth alongside the narrative.
