## [PRESS] inputs (to be superseded at pull time)

## The proxy
Slippage = nominal GDP growth - delivered per-share growth:
- via price (flat-yield assumption): 11.7 - 9.8 = **+1.9pp/yr** (range -1.2 to +4.0 on
  endpoint choice);
- via EPS: 11.7 - 8.8 = **+2.9pp/yr**.
**Central estimate: India slippage ~ +2 to +3 pp/yr** over 2008-2025.

## Where India sits on the ER-D2 line (proxy placement)
Between France (+2.8) and Italy (+3.7); far from the USA (-0.2) and nowhere near Portugal
(+18). Cross-check against the -0.79 slippage-return line: India's real broad-market
return over the window ~ +3.5-4%/yr (9.7% nominal - ~6% CPI [PRESS]) — sits ON the line

than GDP (capital share 36->48%), yet per-share delivery ran 2-3pp BELOW GDP — the wedge
is DILUTION (new listings, QIPs, promoter sales absorbing the growth), not profitability.
