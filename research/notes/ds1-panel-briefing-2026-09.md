**DS1 read (context only).** Three years into the "unresolved arc" (11 of 18 at all-time
debt peaks in 2020, no new completed descents), the market has repriced the ENTIRE panel's
debt-service cost upward — six economies at multi-decade yield highs simultaneously (France
2008, Germany 2011, Netherlands 2011, Australia 2011, Norway 2008, Japan 1999) — while the
dollar LOST ground against 15 of the 17 non-USD economies (all but JPY and CAD). Consistent
with the atlas's two standing frames: the descent has not begun anywhere new (rising rates
with peak debt = the arc steepening, not resolving), and the dollar leg matches the
reserve-currency monograph's slow-diversification profile rather than any collapse. The yen
is the two-way outlier — lowest yield AND the only major depreciation — the closest thing
on the panel to a fiscal-dominance fingerprint (cap the yield, pay in the currency).
Switzerland remains the panel's one hard-money exception at ~0.5%. None of this changes any
verdict: DS1's entry stays a STATE, never a clock, and Tier-C stays reduce-only.
