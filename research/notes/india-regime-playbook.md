## What India's regime systematically pays (each line = a print)
1. **Equities as the core, bought on stress, never chased on inflows.** Weak-INR years:
   -12.6% local / -24.4% USD; the NEXT year +24-25% (CU-D6/D7 — 1998,2009,2014,2023
   systematic). Strong-INR inflow years pay +54% same-year and only +7% next. The
   structural edge is BEING THE LIQUIDITY in the outflow year — exactly Tier-C
   reduce-only + stress deployment.
2. **INR fixed income is India's honest carry** — the anti-Japan trade: the sovereign
   PAYS +1.5-2.5 real (no repression tax at 82% debt, DB-D6 buckets), and RBI-cycle
   easing adds duration gains (L10/L20 states time it). Japan's regime paid duration via
   disinflation; India's pays it via LEVEL.
3. **Gold-INR is the permanent compounder, not just a hedge**: gold + the INR's
   structural PPP drift = double-digit nominal in ALL years (CU-D5: +13.4% worst-INR
   years, +12.0% others; T-series Sharpe 1.19) + the crash premium (~6-9pp, CU-D4/D7)
   exactly when the FX-equity cluster breaks.
4. **The dilution tax is India's repression-analog — stand on the right side of it.**
   India's wedge ~+2-3pp/yr (slippage proxy note; ER-D2 -0.79). The Western repression
   taxed BONDHOLDERS; India's issuance machine taxes GROWTH-STORY BUYERS. The analog of
   "don't hold the repressed bond" is "don't be the QIP/IPO bid when L7 runs hot"; prefer
   delivered-cash-flow equities (ER-D9 a1 +0.84 — delivery is everything).
5. **Do NOT import the leveraged-real-asset trade.** The West's r<g repression subsidy
   (DB-D9: +3-5.7pp spreads) does not exist here: Indian rental yields ~2-3% vs ~8.5%
   loan rates = deeply NEGATIVE carry; leveraged Indian property is a pure
   price-appreciation bet — the opposite of the panel's subsidized spread. India's
   version of "cheap funding" belongs to CORPORATES issuing expensive equity, not to
   leveraged buyers.
