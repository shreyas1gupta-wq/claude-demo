# Part F/H — Harvest and knowledge ledger (atlas 0.3; CONTEXT entry, deliberately thin)

## F — What this entry is allowed to touch

| Consumer | What it gets | What it may NEVER do |
|---|---|---|
| The gold-floor debate (L15) | the real-rate era classification, via the debt monograph's repression gauge — 0.3 adds the 150y context behind it | set numbers |

Rejected uses, named: terminal-multiple assumptions anchored to "the trend"; duration bets on
reversion to it; r-star-based timing of anything (HLW CIs wider than the object). No module, no
tests, no seat — the absence IS the design decision, and this monograph is its documentation.

## H — Ledger

**Established:** the 150y slope (−1.27bp/yr, SR1) sits in Schmelzing's band; the swings dominate
(14x, SR2) — and Rogoff-Rossi-Schmelzing's own re-examination reports the trend is not reliably
detectable in ~150y sub-samples, corroborating SR2 from inside the literature; the 2010s were low
but not unprecedented (27th percentile, SR3).
**Contested, tracked:** which secular-decline theory survived 2022-24 (the chapter's scorecard).
**Unknowable:** the next multi-decade swing's direction — which is the entry's whole point.
