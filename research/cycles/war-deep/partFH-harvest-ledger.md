# Part F/H — Harvest and knowledge ledger (atlas 0.6; REJECTION entry)

## Why there is no data leg
Nothing in the grand-cycle frameworks defines a measurable, pre-registrable object that is not
already held elsewhere: war finance IS the debt arc (0.1, DS1's peaks), monetary-order breaks ARE
0.2's cases, war inflations ARE 0.5's arc starts. The absence of a B-RESULTS section is the
finding.

## H — Ledger
**Established:** the frameworks' retrodictive appeal and their n≈3-5-different-processes problem;
Turchin's 2010 forecast as the one genuine advance prediction, scored both ways; the market
record around major wars (the "war puzzle": returns often positive through wars that don't
destroy the market's home state — survivorship's sharpest lesson); GPR's real vol/tail content
and weak unconditional return predictability.
**Unknowable, permanently:** the next hegemonic transition's schedule. The book holds insurance
against consequences, never bets on calendars.
