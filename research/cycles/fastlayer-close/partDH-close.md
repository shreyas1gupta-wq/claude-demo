# Parts D–H — the fast-layer closer (atlas 5.4 flag / 5.5 reject / 5.6 out-of-scope)

## Part D — Three verdicts that need no new evidence, and the one that gets a grading path

**5.4 (1-month cross-sectional reversal).** The literature is real (Jegadeesh 1990; Lehmann
1990; the liquidity-provision reading in Nagel 2012 — reversal profits are compensation for
making markets when nobody wants to): a genuine risk premium, category (iii) survival
argument. And the atlas verdict is Tier-C flag with ZERO return budget anyway, because the
premium is paid for IMMEDIACY — harvesting it means being the buyer in falling names at
daily speed, which is precisely what a weekly-cadence EOD desk cannot be. Add India's 20bp
STT round trip and the published cost-fragility (the anomaly dies first in every
transaction-cost study), and the flag's only legitimate job is H08-adjacent: informing
entry TIMING of positions the book already wants (don't chase a name up 15% this month).
MR1 (ledger) is the grading path — permanent freeze unless net-of-cost survives both
half-samples plus the decay haircut; even a pass caps consumption at "flag".

**5.5 (weekly/daily seasonals).** REJECT by the same knife as 4.11, one band faster:
day-of-week and expiry-day-drift patterns carry no surviving mechanism at our scale after
costs, and the calendar-pattern archetype is banned by Contract §8. No trial is spent —
CW3/GS1 already demonstrated the omnibus discipline at monthly resolution, and a daily
demonstration would require the bhavcopy vault to prove a negative the register already
prices at zero. The refusal card suffices; a mechanism-bearing claimant (like 4.3's drain
dates — which are NOT seasonals but statute) routes through H58's ops lens instead.

**5.6 (intraday cycles).** OUT OF SCOPE by design, not by verdict: the open/close U-shape
and auction effects are real microstructure, but this is an EOD-data, weekly-cadence program
(CONTRACT §1) — the desk neither measures nor trades intraday, and execution algos inherit
standard participation logic. Out-of-scope differs from reject: no evidence claim is made
either way, and the entry exists so nobody mistakes silence for ignorance.
