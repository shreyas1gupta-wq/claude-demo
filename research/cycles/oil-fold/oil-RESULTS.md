  the global-equity sign, so "supply-flavored oil-up" years are by construction global-down
  years — and India (GF2 loading +0.57) falls in global-down years regardless of what oil is
  doing. The declared conflation does most of the work: this print is consistent with

  2008's annual-averaging artifact (H1 spike + H2 crash nets to an "oil-up year").

  supply/demand/precautionary shocks are separated by structure, not by the equity tape.
