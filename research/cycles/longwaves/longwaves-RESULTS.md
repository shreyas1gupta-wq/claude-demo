## Honest read (written AFTER the print)

- **KW1 — no 45-60y wave in real commodity prices.** Even with the machinery FORCED to skip
  25 years between troughs, the index breaks toward ~26-31y spacings (0 of 5 in the claimed
  window). A 50y wave would need every other one of these troughs to vanish; nothing in the
  data votes for that pairing over any other. Combined with CS1 (8 troughs at 15-21y when
  min_gap is 15), the resolution story is now measured twice: the spacing you "find" tracks
  the min_gap you impose — EXCEPT that no min_gap choice we pre-registered ever lands on
  Kondratieff's band. The wave isn't hiding at another resolution; it isn't there.
- **KW2 — Kondratieff saw something real, and it is already seated elsewhere.** The
  inflation-peak chronology is strikingly SYNCHRONIZED across his own three countries
  (~1920, ~1948-50, ~1981-83, ~2008-13) — generational price waves exist. But their spacings
  run 25-41y (median 36y, 1 of 10 in [45,60]) and their mechanisms differ era by era
  (metallic-standard supply waves, war finance, fiat policy arcs). This is precisely the
  inflation-regimes object (IR1's arcs) and the debt/financial-cycle content the atlas
  already carries: the de-duplication argument against a K-wave seat is now empirical, not
  just architectural. What he called one 50-year wave decomposes into shorter, seated,
  mechanism-named states.
- **Verdict feed:** D07 REJECT stands with the desk's own numbers attached. Both bars
  unmoved; no cell re-tried.
