# Parts D–H — verdict + routing (atlas 2.7; CONTEXT folded into L5/L6)

## Part D — What FP1a/FP1b establish, and the anatomy of an honest pass

FP1a is this project's first study in DEFLATING a pass: the bar was met exactly as registered
(+3.99%/m vs +0.72 base, 7/8 positive) and the dissection then did what dissections are for —
(i) the registered window CONTAINS the result month, so drift and surprise are conflated by
construction (a design flaw caught after the fact, recorded, and fixed in FP-D1's registered
design rather than by re-running); (ii) one global-rebound window carries the pooled mean
(ex-2009 ≈ +1.9%/m); (iii) n=8 is an association sample. The rule extracted for the register:
A PASS IS A CLAIM ABOUT THE REGISTERED CONSTRUCTION, NOT ABOUT THE WORLD — promotion to any
traded use runs through the lane's paper-trade machinery, which for elections scores one
observation per half-decade. FP1b measures what L5 actually uses: windows are LOUDER (median
|move| 5.4% vs 4.2%) and result months are violent both ways (−17.7..+33.6) — scheduling risk
down into the window is rational without any directional view.
