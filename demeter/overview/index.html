What it is

Two engines, one signal

The architecture is deliberately simple; the work is in the signal that decides, every afternoon, which engine runs tomorrow.

Engine One

Levered long index futures

  • S&P 500 and/or NASDAQ futures at a fixed multiplier
  • Variable leverage 2:1 to 3:1 when conviction is high; unlevered or reduced exposure when volatility is elevated but not bearish
  • Active in low-volatility, positive-momentum regimes
Daily signal3:59 PM NY · rules-based, no discretion · detects the transition between calm bull regimes and high-volatility bear or crash regimes
cash on roughly half of trading days
Engine Two

Fully liquid cash

  • Zero equity exposure, earning the risk-free rate
  • Active when the model detects bearish conditions, heightened stress or adverse events that raise downside risk
  • Avoids buying equities at panic-level volatility premiums

    Five academic phenomena the signal exploits

    As described by the manager. The source lists the fifth row under the heading "Volatility Clustering"; its content is the variance risk premium, so it is labelled accordingly here.

    PhenomenonAcademic findingHow Demeter exploits it
    Volatility clusteringHigh-volatility periods cluster together; calm regimes also persist. A single large daily move predicts elevated volatility in subsequent sessions.The signal detects volatility-regime transitions in real time. When high-vol clustering begins, the engine shifts to cash, systematically avoiding the avalanche of losses that follow the first large negative shock.
    Leverage effectEquity price declines mechanically raise financial leverage, amplifying future volatility asymmetrically: volatility rises faster on down-moves than it falls on up-moves.By the time the leverage effect amplifies volatility the engine is typically already in cash. The levered long position is active only when leverage-effect risk is subdued, capturing upside while sidestepping down-move amplification.†
    Mean reversionOver intermediate horizons asset prices exhibit negative serial autocorrelation; overshooting is eventually corrected and the market reverts toward fundamental value.The signal captures oversold conditions preceding mean-reversion rallies, re-entering levered positions at depressed prices. This buy-low dynamic systematically harvests the recovery premium.
    Regime switchingEquity markets alternate between distinct statistical regimes (low-vol bull; high-vol bear; crash) with very different return and correlation properties.The dual-engine architecture is explicitly a regime-switching system: Engine One (levered SPX/NQ) in the low-vol, positive-momentum regime; Engine Two (cash) in the high-vol, negative-momentum regime.
    Variance risk premiumImplied volatility systematically exceeds realised volatility; the spread compensates option sellers for bearing variance risk and expands dramatically in high-fear periods.By sidestepping the market during fear spikes, the engine avoids purchasing equities at panic-level volatility premiums and re-enters only when those premiums have dissipated.

    † The manager's text for this cell begins mid-sentence ("…already in cash"); the opening clause is a reconstruction and should be confirmed with the manager.

    Daily framework

    The four quadrants

    Demeter classifies every trading day by whether the strategy was invested and whether the market rose. Positive days are a minority; the record is built on avoiding losses and amplifying the days that are caught.

    Source and reconciliation

    Monthly returns for the strategy and SPY were transcribed from Demeter's factsheet tables (family-office period Jul 2012 – Jul 2023; commingled fund Aug 2023 – Jun 2026, Share Class B).
