# Appendix D — Program Risk Register

Companion to `docs/MASTER_PLAN.md` (§16 index), `docs/DESIGN.md`, and `research/register/{consistency-audit,decay-redteam}.md` (the source of most Design-category entries below — this register operationalizes their findings into owned, monitored items rather than leaving them as one-time audit prose). 41 risks across six categories. **Likelihood/Impact** = L/M/H, assessed as of 2026-08-31 against the frozen design; re-score at every quarterly review (§8.3) and whenever a trigger fires. **Owner**: P = principal, C = Claude, J = joint (mutual review is the control). **Hook** = the specific config field, validator check, gate, or standing process that is supposed to catch this risk — where the hook does not yet exist, this register states that explicitly as the fix required.
