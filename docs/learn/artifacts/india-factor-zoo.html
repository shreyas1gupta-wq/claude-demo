5 · Consumption

  • What's actually in the book today: the vol-managed WML+HML sleeve (standing book, +1.80%/yr contribution at 15% weight) — the only India factor construction that has cleared a promotion bar.
  • What's a monitor, not a trade: the momentum stand-down gate (Tier-C), the small-vs-large valuation spread (in-sample, no PIT data yet), the winter rebound (IIMA-only support).
  • What's an avoid-list line: post-bear smallcap re-entry (12m minimum); the raw size premium itself is not a buy-and-hold factor in India at all.
  • What's genuinely unknown: quality, growth, the full valuation ladder, and every accrual/ earnings-quality red flag, at the India stock level — all frozen pending the fundamentals handoff.
