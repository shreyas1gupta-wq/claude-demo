A Run Without Deposits

India's shadow-credit cycle: lenders that fund ten-year assets with ninety-day paper, held by mutual funds whose investors can leave daily. When IL&FS defaulted in September 2018, the desk's own test shows the freeze reached the ENTIRE equity market within a year — which is exactly why this entry ships no new seat, no new budget, and one hard-won routing decision: detection lives in funding variables, at L2's speed.

PART 1

The structure that keeps being rebuilt

A deposit run needs depositors. India's 2018 run needed none: NBFCs and housing-finance companies had funded long loan books in the commercial-paper market, debt mutual funds held the paper, and fund investors redeem at will. That chain has a first-mover advantage exactly like a classic bank run — leave before the fund marks the paper down — but with no deposit insurance and no lender of last resort behind it. The deeper lesson is WHY the structure exists: every tightening of bank regulation pushes intermediation to the next lightly regulated form. The fragile node moves; the fragility is conserved. India ran this cycle with deposit-taking NBFCs in the 1990s and CP-funded ones in 2018; the watch list for the next node (co-lending books, FLDG-backed digital lending, microfinance) is a standing question in the monograph, never an answer.

the measured routing argument
Why a failed trial is the entry's best output. If a 12-month equity window cannot isolate the event, all the harvestable edge lives in the gap between when FUNDING variables move (days-weeks: commercial-paper spreads, paper that fails to roll, fund redemptions) and when fundamentals catch up (months). That gap belongs to L2, the fast stress layer. The entry therefore contributes a freeze index — a spread leg and a rollover leg, either one's extreme is an alarm — as INPUT to L2's existing family, under L2's existing budget. Equity-factor detection is recorded in the ledger as a dead family, with its failing print attached.
PART 3

No new seat — and that's the design

composition

How much of credit is NBFC/unsecured → already a leg inside L10 (the credit monograph seated it). Nothing added.

market stress

Drawdowns, vol, breadth → already L2's seat. Nothing added.

funding freeze

CP spread z + rollover-collapse z → new INPUTS offered to L2, acceptance bars registered when the data lands (two-pass rule), reduce-only consequences.

the playbook

The 2018-19 sector-rotation record → a Stage-2 briefing document that arms when the index does. Descriptive, never automatic.

ratings

IL&FS was AAA weeks before default. Ratings enter the stack as post-mortem confirms, never inputs — codified.

A "shadow-credit seat" would be L10's composition leg plus L2's stress seat wearing a third name — double-counting with extra steps. The monograph's deliverable is the routing and the proof of no-double-count, plus the case anatomy (IL&FS at full length, DHFL, Yes Bank, the 1990s purge, the US and China mirrors) that teaches where to point the instruments next time the fragility relocates.
