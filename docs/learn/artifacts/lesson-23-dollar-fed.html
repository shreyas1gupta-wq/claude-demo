PART 2

The number that actually matters

Why the Fed cycle folds on evidence, not just parsimony. DL3 asked the crudest form of the question: do annual changes in the US policy rate explain realized dollar moves? Same-year −0.03, next-year +0.06 — zero. The rate-differential mechanism is real but operates through EXPECTATIONS at higher frequency; a "Fed cycle seat" built on realized policy paths would be empty. L9's US-policy leg therefore reads real-yield LEVELS, and announcement-day trading stays refused (the same refusal the RBI seat made in Lesson 20 — the symmetry is deliberate).
