PART 2

What a desk does with a two-point cycle

A regime is doctrine plus memory: Volcker's disinflation worked as a fiscal-monetary regime change (Sargent's famous reading), and it held for four decades partly because the people who lived through 1979 ran central banks until they didn't. The 2021–23 episode was the regime's first live test in a generation — the "transitory" call failed, the fastest hiking cycle in 40 years followed, and expectations broadly held: the monograph scores both columns without pretending a verdict. India's version: chronic high single digits for three decades, the 2013–16 glide path, then the 2016 MPC framework — our regime change, two breaches since, band retained through 2031.

Rejected uses, named: timing the arc's turn (two data points); "new 1970s" portfolio overhauls; trading inflation prints (81% stickiness means the print is mostly noise around the era). What survives: persistence gauges inside L15, the faster monetary reads in L6, and one registered escalation watch — a persistent breach of India's band while the fiscal-dominance state is ON. That pairing, and only that pairing, escalates anything.
