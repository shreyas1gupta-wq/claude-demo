Nothing to Time, Something to Watch

Factor rotation is the most-marketed idea in quant retail: value's turn, momentum's turn, rotate and win. This entry retires it — the complementarity is already harvested statically and the regime conditioning already lives in a seat. What survives is its dangerous cousin: CROWDING, where copycat capital piles into visible factors until a shock forces everyone through the same exit. India's factor library confirms the exit's depth, kills an imported stylized fact, and sets the monitor's design before its data exists.

PART 1

Why rotation folds

The desk's own numbers (Lessons 3-4) measured value and momentum at −0.4 correlation in India, with the 50/50 blend beating both sleeves on Sharpe — the complementarity is real and it is harvested STATICALLY, by holding both. Timing the leadership would mean out-forecasting the regime seats that already condition the factor book, and the factor-timing literature's honest record ("deceptively difficult" is the polite version) says that game funds the players who refuse it. "Rotation" retires as a label with the evidence attached; nothing to time.

PART 2

Two honest surprises

The skew import dies (CR1a). Every US quant knows "momentum is negatively skewed." India's 33-year library says: not unconditionally (+0.05, the same as size; VALUE is the positively-skewed one at +0.60). No contradiction with the momentum monograph's crash finding — that finding was CONDITIONAL (bear-market-then-rally windows), and a conditional crash tail coexists with zero unconditional skew when normal times are right-skewed. The refined sentence: India's momentum danger is REGIME-LOCAL — which is exactly what the seated crash_guard implements. An unconditional de-risking of momentum would have been the wrong medicine; the fail is the evidence for the design.
The named episode is invisible (CR2). The atlas cites a mid-2025 quant unwind. India's monthly momentum factor shows NOTHING — no 2025 month even reaches −2σ. Either the episode was a US/implementation story, or unwinds live at daily and intramonth granularity that monthly academic factors cannot see. Both readings force the same design conclusion: the crowding monitor CANNOT be built from factor returns. Its legs are the atlas's own observables — smart-beta AUM growth-rates and factor-valuation percentiles — with a comomentum leg when stock-level data lands. The candidate is instrumented with the right sensors from day one because the wrong sensor was tested first.
PART 3

The monitor design

accumulation

AMFI smart-beta/factor-fund AUM growth-rate percentiles (momentum, low-vol, quality funds) — copycat capital is countable in India's fund data, monthly, free.

valuation

Each factor's own value-spread percentile (the L8 machinery pointed at the factor's long book) — crowding shows as the factor getting expensive against itself.

comomentum

Abnormal within-factor correlation (the Lou-Polk gauge) once stock-level returns are vaulted — the direct crowd-thermometer, registered as a design.

consumption

Tier-C, reduce-only monitors on momentum/low-vol/quality sleeves. Watching accumulation, never predicting the shock — the unwind's trigger and date stay unknowable, as everything in this atlas does.
