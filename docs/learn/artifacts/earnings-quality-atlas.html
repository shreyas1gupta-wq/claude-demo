1 · The accrual-divergence battery (EQ-D1)

Composite (equal-weight of all three)+2.68 (large-cap)more negative than any single partMISS
The lesson: two of three Beneish-style ingredients ran backwards on this panel — plausibly a mean-reversion signature (margin-compressed, more-levered firms rebounding) rather than the fraud-adjacent reading the textbook formula intends, or a rank-difference construction artifact that conflates "declining from a high base" with "declining further from an already-low one." Averaging them with the one ingredient that DID work didn't average out the noise — it diluted the signal. A composite red-flag score is only as good as its worst-signed component; the fix is to verify each one's own sign on the target data before combining, never port a textbook multi-factor score blind. This is the single most transferable lesson for the India rebuild.

The "good" (low-redflag) half of cheap stocks did WORSE at 12m — inverted, exactly like VAL-D2's within-cheap conditioning and VAL-D3's trap filters, and SC-D3a's blindness to the 2018-19 smallcap unwind. Same mechanism each time: a no-delisting panel deletes the failures a trap filter exists to avoid, so filtering removes the survivor bounce instead of the trap. Graded as a third artifact sighting, not a fourth trap-filter failure — the real test needs deaths in the data.

5 · Edge candidates & the India queue

  • Carry forward now: the TATA_proxy avoid-list line (high earnings-vs-cash divergence, avoid) — cheap, robust, negative everywhere tested.
  • Do not carry forward: margin-decline and leverage-increase as red-flag inputs in their current rank-difference form; the mechanically-combined composite in any form.
  • India-registrable on arrival: true Sloan TATA + cash-conversion + a properly-signed reduced Beneish (5 of 8 components) + the promoter-pledge trap filter, tested WITH real deaths in the data via the delisted registry — the first honest trap-filter test this program will have run.
