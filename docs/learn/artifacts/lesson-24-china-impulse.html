PART 1

The pulse and its shadow

The China credit impulse — the change in the flow of new credit relative to GDP — is a second-derivative object built for a policy-driven credit system: easing decision → credit surge → construction orders → metals demand → emerging-market terms of trade. Since 2008 the record shows four to five such pulses, each ending in a deleveraging campaign. India feels them twice: as an importer's input-cost headwind and as a metals-sector tailwind — the same two-signed harvest as the commodity seat, which is why H54 is designed as an ENRICHMENT input to the global seat rather than a standalone driver.

PART 2

What a proxy can and cannot license

The proxy discipline, stated once for every future blocked dataset. When the real variable is unreachable (China's TSF behind blocked hosts, no mirror found), the desk may measure a shadow — but the confound list is written into the registration, the passes license candidacy only, and the REAL test (does the true TSF impulse add incremental loading inside the global factor, purged and pooled?) is registered to run when the data lands on the principal's machine. The shadow then stays on as a cross-check leg: two independent shadows beat one. What is never allowed: promoting the shadow to a signal because its trials passed — that would be laundering identification through pre-registration theater.
PART 3

The open question that bounds everything

The case record's honest ending: the pulse machinery is changing. The property era — the channel that made every Chinese easing a metals event — is winding down, and the 2022-26 pulses transmit visibly weaker to commodities. If the next China runs on manufacturing exports instead of construction, its India transmission may FLIP SIGN: from commodity complementarity (China-up = metals-up = two-signed for India) to export competition (China-up = pressure on Indian manufacturing). The entry doesn't resolve that — it registers the watch, notes that the cadence itself (3-4 years, n≈5) may be dissolving with its driver, and leaves H54 exactly where a candidate belongs: instrumented, bounded, and waiting for its data.
