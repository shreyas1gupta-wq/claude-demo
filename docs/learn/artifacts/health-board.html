1 · The story in sixty seconds

This program built a multi-horizon cycle model for Indian markets (NIFTY 750 + gold + debt, three books) by working the entire Cycle Atlas — 55 rows across six bands, from 200-year debt arcs to intraday microstructure — one monograph at a time, with every claim pre-registered before its data ran. The deepest result is negative and load-bearing: markets have persistence, not periodicity. Six clock claims were tested; five failed, the sixth passed and was refused promotion, and the control group (ENSO — the planet's own oscillator) still failed the bar. Everything the desk trades is a STATE with a half-life, read for permission, never prediction. The atlas is complete; the machinery (states → regime score → risk buckets, with walk-forward adjudication and CI-enforced promotion law) is built and tested; 164 pre-registered run cells have printed, ~60 of them on authenticated mirror data across ten vaults. THE MIRROR FRONTIER IS NOW EXHAUSTED (3 Sep 2026): every remote-reachable series is vaulted to its limit and the dead ends are probed and recorded — what remains is gated on your machine's data pulls and the five decisions listed at the end of this page.
