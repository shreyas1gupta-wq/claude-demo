1 · The story in sixty seconds

This program built a multi-horizon cycle model for Indian markets (NIFTY 750 + gold + debt, three books) by working the entire Cycle Atlas — 55 rows across six bands, from 200-year debt arcs to intraday microstructure — one monograph at a time, with every claim pre-registered before its data ran. The deepest result is negative and load-bearing: markets have persistence, not periodicity. Six clock claims were tested; five failed, the sixth passed and was refused promotion, and the control group (ENSO — the planet's own oscillator) still failed the bar. Everything the desk trades is a STATE with a half-life, read for permission, never prediction. Since the atlas closed, the desk has been building on that spine: Track T found the overnight premium (+24%/yr, calm days only); the option program produced the standing book — the 65/20/15 collar-stacked book at +11.46 CAGR (TR ~+12.76) / −11.36 maxDD — plus two optimization grids that honestly refused their own train winners out-of-sample; the macro batteries (expected returns, GDP, currency, debt, credit+inflation) turned valuation and inflation into STATES for expectations, never point forecasts; Track FUN found the phase inversion at three scales; and the quality-growth track caught the EW-survivor junk artifact and ended at the moderation principle. 1,140 pre-registered run cells have now printed across 80 ledger entries and 14 authenticated vaults, policed by an adversarial audit that withdrew one headline and booked eight corrections in public.
