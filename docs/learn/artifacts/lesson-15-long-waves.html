The Fifty-Year Prophecy

Kondratieff waves, tested on Kondratieff's own object with the desk's own machinery: the generational price waves he saw are real — synchronized across his three countries — but they run 25-41 years with a different mechanism each era, and the claimed 45-60-year band is empty at every resolution we pre-registered. The wave is rejected with numbers; Perez's surge framework survives as an unbudgeted red-team lens on the AI decade.

PART 1

The claim, and why it never dies

In 1925 Nikolai Kondratieff published evidence of 50-odd-year waves in English, French and American price levels, interest rates and trade. He hedged carefully; the label did not. Schumpeter built a cathedral on it, Forrester rebuilt it in systems dynamics, and every crisis since 1929 has been "the K-wave winter" to someone. Kondratieff himself was shot in 1938 — Soviet orthodoxy required capitalism's crises to be terminal, and his said they were cyclical. The idea's durability has a simpler mechanism than its content: a 50-year prophecy is irrefutable inside a career. Nobody trades a full wave; everybody can narrate one.

A desk doesn't argue with folklore — it pre-registers the folklore's own numbers. Two trials, bars written before anything ran: KW1 hunts a 45-60y wave in 165 years of real commodity prices (the machinery from Lessons 13-14, minimum gap forced to 25 years so long waves get every chance); KW2 tests price waves — Kondratieff's actual object — on his own three countries' full price histories.

PART 3

Where the true fragments already live

The rejection is not a claim that nothing long exists — the atlas already TRADES long objects. It is a claim that the K-wave is six seated states wearing one mystical label:

price waves

The KW2 chronology IS the inflation-regime arcs (Lesson 9) — seated, with era-specific mechanisms named instead of averaged away.

debt build

Multi-decade leverage arcs → the debt supercycle seat (Lesson 5).

mid cycle

Credit-property amplification → L12 (Lesson 12).

commodities

Real price arcs, measured at 15-21y → H53's state (Lesson 14).

"winter"

Crisis clustering → fast stress + hedge scheduling, dated by states, never prophecy.

tech manias

Innovation-cluster capex → atlas 1.5, below — kept as a LENS, refused as a clock.

PART 4

Perez: the one long-wave idea worth keeping, unbudgeted

Carlota Perez claims a recurring SEQUENCE, not a period: financial capital funds an installation frenzy (canal mania, railway mania, dotcom), a turning point crashes it, and production capital deploys the technology into a golden age. Five observations, flexible dating — so it earns no state and no budget. But as a set of questions about the AI-capex decade it is the sharpest lens the desk has, and the monograph fixes three of them as standing quarterly red-team questions: who is the railway and who is the over-paying shipper in AI capex; what would a FUNDING-channel turning point look like and which existing states would pull permissions first; and is India positioned as 1860s Bombay — the funding periphery of someone else's mania — or as a deployment beneficiary. Stage-2 questions, never Stage-1 inputs. The prophecy stays rejected; the interrogation stays scheduled.
