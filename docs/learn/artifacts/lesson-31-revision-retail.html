PART 1

The fourth verdict type

The register now holds four ways to say no, each with its own honesty requirement:

evidence reject

The claim fails its pre-registered bars (war cycles, K-waves, the five clocks). Requirement: the failing print, attached forever.

fold

The object is real and already seated under another name (Juglar → L10/L11; rotation → L8). Requirement: the address, so the label can't re-enter as new alpha.

context

Real, datable, and not worth budget (the business cycle, Perez). Requirement: the budget-of-zero argued, the chronology kept.

data reject

NEW with 3.5: the claim may be TRUE and the desk declines it because its inputs violate the free-source rule. Requirement: COST ACCOUNTING — the forgone alpha stated plainly — plus a concrete revisit trigger (a free consensus source appearing), checked at the annual loop.

Why keep a rule that costs alpha? Three reasons on the record: auditability (every number in this project can be recomputed by anyone from public sources — including the people this desk will train); crowding (paid-feed edges concentrate exactly where the most capital fishes); and the Contract's discipline that a rule bent once for a good signal will be bent again for a bad one. The revision cycle's proxies from free data — result-day surprises, guidance tone — measure DIFFERENT objects than the revision path, and pretending otherwise would be worse than the rejection.
PART 2

The crowd you can count for free

India's retail wave is the rare sentiment object with complete public instrumentation: demat account counts (roughly 4 crore to over 16 crore across the 2020-24 surge), the retail share of index-option turnover, and — uniquely — the regulator's own loss studies, which found ~9 in 10 individual F&O traders losing money. That last document is also the MECHANISM's evidence: new-cohort enthusiasm is pro-cyclical and mean-reverts after losses — alpha decay operating on people. The 2024-25 SEBI curbs (lot sizes, expiry rationalization) are the institutional confirmation, the same pattern the issuance seat's SEBI citation established: a regulator acting IS a froth reading.

PART 3

The migration law

The wave's history is a venue history: cash-market cohorts in 2004-07, F&O cohorts in 2021-24, and whatever the next un-curbed venue turns out to be. Like shadow credit's fragile node and issuance's vehicles, the crowd MIGRATES when regulated — so the counter's category audit is annual, and the monitor watches accumulation wherever it moves without ever forecasting enthusiasm. The desk's posture, one line: count the crowd, price the exit, refuse the prophecy.
