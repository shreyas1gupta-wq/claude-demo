PART 1

Standing still beat rotating (T3), and a shape without a certificate (T4)

Dual momentum between the NIFTY and rupee-gold — the classic rotation — lost to the 50/50 static blend on BOTH axes at both lookbacks: lower net Sharpe AND deeper drawdowns in the clean window. The registered prior expected the Sharpe loss but not the drawdown loss; the miss is booked, and the mechanism is instructive — a relative rule forces one speed onto two assets that trend at different speeds (TS1 measured 3 months for equity, 12 for gold), so it buys timing risk while selling the rebalancing premium. The quiet star: INR-gold's own Sharpe printed at 1.19 over 2007-2026 — the measured base under the books' standing gold allocations. T4 then found the low-volatility anomaly's exact silhouette in India (beta 0.67, drawdown 24% vs 34%, Sharpe 1.45 vs 1.34) with the alpha t-stat at 1.88 — under the bar. Because survivorship biases AGAINST low-vol here, the declared one-way makes this fail INCONCLUSIVE rather than a death: the point-in-time data decides, and nothing enters the factor library on a near-miss.

PART 3

The drift that vanishes in stress, and the fingerprint that wasn't there

T1b asked the operational question T1's +24%/yr overnight discovery demanded: does the drift survive stress? No — in the frozen crisis windows the overnight mean collapses to statistical nothing, and the REAL surprise is where the damage lives: in-episode INTRADAY returns run −66%/yr (t=−3.3). The prior said global bad news lands overnight; India's crisis damage is a local-session event, and the mechanism miss is booked. The execution playbook that ships: buy-at-close on calm days (the accrual is real), and de-risk fires may execute at the next open in stress without sacrificing measurable drift. T6 then tested the turn-of-month calendar — real, +7.2bp/day, p=0.025 — for the SIP-era amplification the inelastic-flows candidate would predict. The fingerprint is ABSENT: the premium SHRANK from +12.2 to +3.8bp/day across the 2015 SIP break. As the registration promised, that's booked AGAINST H61's index-level motivation — the aggregate-multiplier test survives untouched, but it can no longer cite this bump. A desk that registers what would count against its own new favorite, then prints it, is the register working.
