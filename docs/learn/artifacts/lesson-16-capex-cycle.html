The Seat That Argues Against Itself

India's capex cycle gets a working module in the ladder — and the honest headline is that its own evidence trial failed. This lesson is about the rarest design move in the stack: a seat whose weakness is measured, stated, and then engineered into its algebra, so it can only ever subtract risk. Overbuilds take years to repair; hot capex prints add nothing.

PART 1

The claim, and the trial that failed

The capex story is the accelerator plus time-to-build: tight capacity → orders → five-to- eight-year gestation → everyone's capacity arrives together → glut → years of balance-sheet repair. India has lived it twice at full scale (1994-97 → the NPA years; 2003-11 → the twin-balance-sheet decade). India's own official series are blocked from this desk, so the atlas's pre-written clause governed: evidence via analogues — eighteen countries of investment/GDP history against a century of real equity returns, bars registered before anything ran.

PART 3

The clamp: engineering a measured weakness

Put the three results together. The state doesn't order forward returns (IN1 fail, IN3 non-monotone). The hot extreme carries a mild penalty (top quintile below bottom). Repair after overbuilds is real and slow (IN2). A seat like that must never be allowed to ADD bullishness — especially not inside the shared macro-credit budget, where a hot capex print co-occurring with hot credit would double-count the same euphoria. So L11's contribution is min(0, state): silent through normal times and booms, subtracting regime score only in measured overbuild extremes. The raw state still flows, unclamped, to one place where weak-but-real information earns its keep — capital-goods and infra sector tilt confirmation, capped at Tier C.

The module ships with that algebra tested: three legs with India's real staggered starts (GFCF from the 1950s, IIP capital goods from 1994, OBICUS utilization from 2008) degrade gracefully with the leg count exposed, the clamp preserves NaN and never goes positive, and the planted accelerator economy fires early and collapses at the turn — the impulse dynamic, verified now on a third fixture. Machinery is not evidence: the docstring states the tier, the ledger states the fail, and graduation waits on the one test that matters — the purged backtest on India's own three legs once they're pulled.
