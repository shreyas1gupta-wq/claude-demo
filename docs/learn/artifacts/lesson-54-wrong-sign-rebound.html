The Rebound That Changes Sign

Three questions from the principal — earnings revisions, earnings surprises, and what predicts small-vs-large — met twenty pre-registered cells and three literature dossiers in one day. One prior died, one gate passed, and the best-known size trade in American markets turned out to transfer to India with the wrong sign. Every number below is a booked print from the trial ledger (entries ES-D1, SC-D1, SC-D2, SC-D3); literature numbers carry [LIT].

1 · Revisions: the data verdict stands, so we tested the proxy — and it failed honestly

India has no free point-in-time analyst-consensus history — that verdict (Atlas 3.5, DATA-REJECT) is unchanged, and the dossier work re-confirmed it: the revisions literature is five decades deep (Givoly-Lakonishok → Chan-Jegadeesh-Lakonishok → Gleason-Lee [LIT]), but the data to run it in India is paid-only. What CAN be tested free is the analyst-free substitute: fundamental momentum — rank migration in reported EPS (Novy-Marx 2015 [LIT]). ES-D1 ran it on the US firm panel (1,207 stocks, 1999-2019) with the prior at +2-6%/yr. The print:

Diagnosis, not despair: the negative ladder is the EW-survivor reversal artifact this desk already caught once (QG-D2) — EPS-rank collapses mark beaten-down names whose no-delisting, equal-weighted returns then bounce. The one mechanism-consistent pocket: fundamental improvement pays +4-5%/yr only where price momentum has not yet moved (e5's low-momentum bins — exactly the Novy-Marx nuance [LIT]). Recorded, not consumed: it sits inside a failed gate on a survivor panel. The India route that remains is the clean one — a point-in-time PEAD event study on the fundamentals handoff's filing_date column, registrable the day that vault authenticates.

The growth gap runs the other way — months when small caps were out-growing large caps preceded MORE small outperformance (T3−T1 +9.4pp, corr +0.22 at 36m): a weak momentum-flavored conditioner, not a contrarian one. And the dossier adds the structural warning: wide spreads can forecast real cash-flow gaps rather than mispricing (Cohen-Polk-Vuolteenaho [LIT]) — the decomposition any India version must survive.

Gate PASSED → the India ask goes live. The NSE index P/E-P/B history row is now on the runsheet (with the 2021 standalone→consolidated splice break named in advance), and the dossier's caution stands: NSE's published smallcap P/E is only partially point-in-time, so the clean fallback instrument is a price-only smallcap/largecap relative series.

3 · The headline: the post-bear small rebound inverts in India

SC-D2 and SC-D3 ran the same four time-series cells on a century of US SMB (1926-2024) and three decades of India SMB (IIMA, 1993-2025). Two US facts printed: size momentum existed only before Banz published it (+7.96%/12m pre-1981, then −0.4 and −0.7 — the third era-fragility instance this register has booked in two days), and the post-bear small rebound is real. Then India:

Next-12m SMB after a DOWN market yearUSIndia
after a down year+6.18%−12.31%
after an up year+1.03%−0.20%
the gap+5.15pp — the rebound−12.11pp — the INVERSION

In the US, small caps are the high-beta recovery asset: the year after a bear year is their best state. In India, the year after a bear year is when smallcaps keep losing to largecaps — consistent with everything this register already knows about the segment (uncompensated beta at factor level, stress that keeps arriving after the event, the risk-off identifiability lag). The only India timing shape found: after a multi-year smallcap winter (bottom tercile of trailing-36m SMB), the next year prints +10.32% — a watch, never a tilt. The IIMA regime map passed its own sanity gate: the 2018 categorization-reform peak and the 2023-24 froth are both visible at the right dates, and the series' full-period mean (−2.82%/yr) matches the TL-D2 verdict on an independent construction.

The playbook line this bought: after an Indian bear year, do not add smallcap risk for at least the next twelve months. Tier-C, reduce-only, like everything else in this segment. The rebound trade you learned from American history has the wrong sign here — that one sentence is what twenty cells were for.

4 · What the dossiers add

Three literature dossiers shipped with the prints (research/notes/es-dossiers/): revisions must never be conflated with forecast dispersion (an opposite-signed, short-sale-constraint effect [LIT]); PEAD did not die, it migrated to small/illiquid names where arbitrage capital won't follow — exactly this desk's Aggressive-book habitat and its highest-cost segment at once [LIT]; India's staggered 45-60-day filing calendar means any PEAD sleeve needs an explicit days-since-announcement state variable, not a uniformly-fresh universe; and small-cap headline growth is routinely diluted away per-share (Fama-French [LIT]) — any India small-growth signal must be built dilution-adjusted, which is precisely what the fundamentals handoff's shares-outstanding history will allow.
