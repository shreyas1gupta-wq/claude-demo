## A. What the business cycle actually is (for a desk)

1. **The object is the growth cycle, not the classical cycle.** Advanced economies since
   the 1980s — and India in its entirety outside 1957/65/72/79/2020 — rarely post
   level-contractions; what recurs is growth decelerating below trend. [BOOKED Atlas
   2.3; LIT Mintz 1969, Aguiar-Gopinath 2007 "The Cycle Is the Trend"]
2. **Bands, never clocks.** Every claimed fixed period (Kitchin 40m, Juglar ~9y, Kuznets
   ~18y, the property clock) failed the program's five-band clock sweep — zero surviving
   fixed periods; the mechanisms live on as bands (inventory, capex/credit ~3-6y,
   financial cycle 10-20y). [BOOKED 19-kitchin-juglar.md]
3. **The lead-lag order that survives measurement:** the MARKET leads GDP by ~1 year and
   no further (5:1 direction ratio); profits lead moderately; IP is coincident; credit
   does NOT lead the real cycle in phase at home (GDP leads credit 16/18) — credit's real
   power is multi-year CRISIS probability, and FUN-D8 m3 confirmed the same horizon
   separation from the return side (credit booms don't dent t+1 returns). [BOOKED
   ER-D8/GDP-D1/BC2/FUN-D8; LIT Schularick-Taylor 2012, Baron-Xiong 2017]
4. **Turning points are asymmetric.** Troughs are sharp (V), peaks rounded; downturns
   diffuse fast across series, recoveries slowly ("jobless recoveries"). So
   entering-contraction is detectable sooner than entering-recovery [LIT Neftçi 1984,
   Sichel 1993] — and the market already trades this: price bottoms ~10m BEFORE the
   earnings trough in 75% of US episodes since 1871, but does NOT reliably lead peaks
   [BOOKED FUN-D2 e2/e3]. Act fast on identified contractions (as a buyer of the
   forward year), slowly on identified recoveries.
5. **THE BAR (non-negotiable):** any candidate leading indicator must beat the market's
   own ~1-year lead — an indicator that forecasts GDP but lags price restates what price
   knows. Nothing on the published record reliably clears it (money growth, curve,
   permits, orders, hours, claims all lead GDP by less-or-equal). [BOOKED bar; LIT
   ranking, dossier A §3]

## B. Global: the measured regime doctrine

**Phases (FUN-D1, JST 17 countries, real-time honest):** next-year real equity —
RECOVERY +8.4% > CONTRACTION +7.1% > EXPANSION +5.0% > SLOWDOWN +2.5% (post-1950: CONT
+12.0 / SLOW +1.2). Same-year returns order the OPPOSITE way: the phase pays
concurrently; an identified phase is already priced. Contraction WITH a financial crisis
= the strongest entry (+13.8%). Bonds best in below-trend phases; housing is the
pro-cyclical laggard. **Inflation outranks the growth quadrant** — high inflation
roughly halves next-year equity in every phase. [BOOKED]

**Monetary regime (FUN-D8):** falling short rates → next-year equity +8.0% vs +3.3%
rising (bonds 3.0/1.7) — the one regime label that is unambiguous and real-time
[BOOKED; converges with LIT Jensen-Johnson-Mercer]. Curve slope ranks equity
monotonically (flat/inverted +3.0 → steep +8.1) ON TOP of recession odds (42%/28%) —
**flagged NEW vs the booked recession-odds-only doctrine; consumption gated on an
India/monthly verification** [BOOKED, flagged]. Real-rate level ranks everything
(equity +3.6→+8.3, bonds −0.2→+6.7 LO→HI): low real rates = repression state = low real
returns everywhere [BOOKED; DB-arc mechanism]. **The seasons table:** post-1950 the only
negative cell of phase×rate-direction is SLOWDOWN×HIKING (−5.2%/yr); best are
CONTRACTION×CUTTING (+13.7) and RECOVERY×HIKING (+11.4). [BOOKED]

**The earnings cycle (FUN-D2, US 1871-2023):** 16 real-earnings recessions (~1/decade),
median −34%, worst −92% (2007-09); median 25m down + 34m recovery. Below-trend/falling
earnings → HIGHER next-12m returns (+5.4/+5.6 vs +3.2/+2.9), concentrated in the cheap
half (+8.8%); mechanism = earnings anti-persistence (AR(1) −0.11); the level signal
decays post-1950. Dividends 2.7x smoother; fall only 16% as much in E-recessions.
[BOOKED]

**What the literature adds that we have NOT yet measured** (each with a real-time-honest
design available — dossier C): trend-following as the standout inflation-surge strategy
and broad commodities (not gold specifically) as the top surge asset [LIT Neville et al.
2021 — our CI killer cell tested only gold]; the Excess Bond Premium as an
incremental-lead credit conditioner [LIT Gilchrist-Zakrajšek 2012]; BAB/funding-tightness
[LIT Frazzini-Pedersen 2014]; the momentum-crash state signature on IIMA WML [LIT
Daniel-Moskowitz 2016 — our sleeve is already vol-managed, the fix their literature
prescribes].

**Known failures — never spend cells:** the Fed model [LIT Asness 2003]; the
unconditional size premium; the naive rotation clock [LIT Stangl et al. — our FUN-D3
killed the India analog independently]; utilities/staples as universal hedges (they are
bond proxies in rate shocks); low-vol as unconditional alpha (our own REFUTED print);
ex-post phase dating of any kind (vintage stamps are non-negotiable).

## C. India: the separate animal

**Character** [dossier B]: a growth-cycle economy (one modern classical contraction:
COVID FY21); trend break at 1991 (Hamilton filter handles it, HP would not — CONTRACT
§8 vindicated); monsoon now a second-order channel working through rural demand and the
~40% food weight in CPI; the 2003-19 credit boom-bust (twin balance sheet) is India's
cleanest domestic credit cycle; demonetization/GST created FORMALIZATION level-shifts
exactly when the modern indicator era begins — early GST-era indicator years carry trend,
not cycle. **No official dating authority exists** — Dua-Banerji (ECRI-style), NIPFP
Bry-Boschan dating (~4-6y growth cycles [LIT, low confidence]), RBI CLI work; all Tier
B/C. India alone fails the ≥4-complete-cycles bar — pool globally, condition locally.

**Transmission to NIFTY earnings — five channels** [dossier B]: bank credit (→ capital
goods/cement/steel; FUN-D6's object), rural demand (monsoon→2W/tractor→FMCG), government
capex (pre-announced, counter-cyclical since ~2014), **the export/IT channel which is a
US-cycle exposure in an India wrapper** (never classify IT as a domestic-cycle sector —
FUN-D3's kill of IT-as-forward-hedge is this fact), and oil imports (~85% dependence;
sign OPPOSITE to exporter-economy commodity literature).

**India sectoral doctrine so far** [BOOKED FUN-D3/SEC-D6/SEC-D7, one-way]: defensives
protect DURING the fall, lag AFTER stress is identifiable (both halves); PSU banks
chronically lag in every calm state; crisis safety = balance sheet > demand curve >
revenue currency; leverage is sector-level negative convexity, surviving-stock call
option. The India-specific published literature on cycle-conditioned assets is thin to
absent — FUN-D4/D7 will likely CONSTITUTE the primary literature.

## D. The conditions that can pay (ranked, with status)

Real-time honest, ranked by evidence quality × actionability for the books:

1. **Post-stress entry (the inversion, three scales)** [BOOKED FUN-D1/D2/D3 + OP-D1
   f1/f2]: identified contraction/crisis years, VIX top-decile days, depressed+cheap
   earnings states → buy the forward 6m-1y. Already expressed in the book (stand-down/
   re-arm, post-spike doctrine); the fundamentals arc extends the same law to annual
   scale. Status: LIVE (the book's machinery).
2. **The avoid-state: hiking into a slowdown** [BOOKED FUN-D8 m8, −5.2%/yr post-1950,
   the only negative season] — an exposure-trim condition candidate for the core's
   state layer. Status: NEEDS an India/monthly verification leg (repo direction ×
   India growth phase) before touching `expo`; pre-register as FUN-D9 when IIP lands.
3. **Rate-direction conditioning** [BOOKED FUN-D8 m1 +8.0/+3.3; LIT Jensen-Johnson
   replicated] — the cleanest real-time regime label. India analog = repo trend; the
   most defensible first India design after the pulls.
4. **Curve slope as a return ranker** [BOOKED FUN-D8 m2, FLAGGED NEW] — monotone
   +3.0/+6.4/+8.1 on JST; gated on India/monthly verification (G-sec term spread).
5. **Inflation regime overrides growth phase** [BOOKED FUN-D1 p9 + CI killer cell;
   LIT Neville et al. concur, adding trend-following/broad commodities as the untested
   legs] — already doctrine; the open cell is a broad-commodity leg beside gold.
6. **Credit as a crisis dial, not a return dial** [BOOKED FUN-D8 m3 + CI arc; LIT
   S-T/B-X/MSV] — RBI credit growth vs excess-credit thresholds sizes TAIL protection
   (ladder tenor/moneyness states), never next-year exposure. FUN-D6's frame.
7. **Earnings-trough anticipation** [BOOKED FUN-D2 e2] — never wait for earnings
   confirmation at bottoms; at India level FUN-D7 (needs NIFTY EPS history) tests
   whether the 10m lead reproduces.
8. **Sector rotation by identified phase** — REFUTED forward in India one-way [BOOKED
   FUN-D3]; contested globally [LIT Stangl]. Only the monetary-regime version (Conover
   et al. line) has a replicated, real-time-honest record — test on NSE sectoral
   indices × repo direction, not on growth phases. Status: gated on the sectoral-index
   pull.
