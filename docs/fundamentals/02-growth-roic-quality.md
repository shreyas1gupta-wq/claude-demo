## A. The canon in six sentences

1. **ROE is leverage-flattered** (DuPont: margin × turnover × leverage); **ROCE**
   half-cleans it (EBIT numerator, dirty denominator); **ROIC vs WACC** is the
   value-creation lens [LIT McKinsey]; **gross profitability** is deliberately crude and
   therefore hardest to game [LIT Novy-Marx 2013].
2. **Growth creates value only when ROIC > WACC** — NPV scales with spread × growth,
   never growth alone; any screen ranking growth without the spread reads half the
   equation.
3. **Returns on capital fade toward WACC** (~half the spread in ~10y [LIT McKinsey]);
   persistence requires a NAMED moat mechanism, and the base rates say decade-long
   high growth from a large base is a low-single-digit-% event [LIT Mauboussin].
4. **Earnings quality = the persistence hierarchy**: cash flows persist, accruals
   reverse [LIT Sloan 1996]; the modern unification is CASH-based operating
   profitability, which dominates both gross profitability and accruals [LIT Ball et
   al. 2016] — the single highest-value construction once firm data lands.
5. Quality screening is **conditional on valuation regime** (F-score for cheap stocks,
   G-score for expensive [LIT Piotroski 2000, Mohanram 2005]) — never one universal
   signal set.
6. The traps: peak-ROCE cyclical screens buy commodity tops; quality at any price is
   its own valuation risk; Ind-AS 116 (~2019) breaks Indian ratio comparability;
   banks get ROE + Tier-1, never ROIC.

## B. The one sentence the edge hangs on

**The market overpays for GROWTH (promised, extrapolated — assets, sales, narrative)
and underpays for PROFITABILITY (realized, hard to fake).** [LIT: asset growth −8%/yr
Cooper-Gulen-Schill 2008 + CMA corroboration; chased glamour −10-11%/yr LSV 1994; vs
gross profitability +4-5%/yr Novy-Marx; QMJ +5-6%/yr across ~24 markets.] Growth-in-
profitability predicts positively (it confirms quality); growth-in-capital-deployed
predicts negatively (it promises scale). This is mechanistically the same physics as
our booked macro growth-negative doctrine — dilution and competition eat promised
growth at every scale [BOOKED GDP/ER arcs].

**Our own aggregate print joins it [BOOKED QG-D1]:** the celebrated Arnott-Asness
"high payout → higher subsequent growth" replicates on 152 years (+4.0 vs +1.8%/yr
next-10y real-E growth, survives post-1950) — but the q5 mechanism cell shows
high-payout months are depressed-earnings months 78% of the time (dividends 2.7x
smoother [BOOKED FUN-D2]), and the gap INVERTS within non-depressed months. Aggregate
payout is a repackaged earnings-state variable. Do not spend cells on aggregate-payout
timing.

## C. First-world / US evidence (what replicates, what decayed)

**Replicates and survives the factor-zoo bar (t>3 families):** profitability broadly
(Novy-Marx GP, FF5 RMW, q-model ROE), the investment/asset-growth direction (CMA + CGS,
cross-methodology agreement), QMJ across ~24 developed markets (smaller/noisier
per-country, pooled Sharpe higher), quality-value complementarity (near-orthogonal —
the combination roughly doubles value's Sharpe), size-controlling-for-junk (small+
quality real, junky smalls drag the raw premium [LIT Asness et al. 2018]).

**Decayed or superseded:** Sloan accruals (~10%/yr original → concentrated in
small/illiquids, superseded by cash-based OP); F-score's ~23%/yr (within-value,
1976-96, small-cap) attenuates hard elsewhere; Magic Formula's book numbers never
survived replication/live vehicles; the average published anomaly loses ~58%
post-publication [LIT McLean-Pontiff 2016]; ~2/3 of ~450 anomalies fail careful
replication [LIT Hou-Xue-Zhang 2020].

**Regime facts to carry:** quality is defensive in downturns (the Safety pillar) —
complements but does not replace our post-stress-entry timing doctrine; the PRICE of
quality predicts QMJ's own forward return (cheap quality → higher returns) — and
quality got expensive post-popularization [LIT]; Japan kills value and momentum but
quality survives muted; the junk SHORT leg is the capacity-constrained side.

## D. India (separate, as always)

**The evidence base is thin and era-scarred.** The NSE Quality indices' outperformance
is mostly PRE-LAUNCH BACKFILL on restated financials — only the live post-launch
segments count, and the newest ones live entirely inside the 2021-24 quality drawdown.
The coffee-can/compounder decade (2010-20) followed by the 2021-24 reversal [LIT] is
the era-concentration warning in its sharpest form: admit quality regime-conditionally
or not at all. The vaulted IIMA factor library has NO quality factor (confirmed:
SMB/HML/WML only) — India quality is data-gated end to end.

**India's real quality axes are governance-shaped, not ratio-shaped:** promoter stake
(~45-50% median) and its changes; promoter PLEDGING (Zee, ADAG, DHFL, Yes Bank all
pledged heavily pre-collapse — the pre-registered signal is pledge-acceleration ×
falling price, never level alone); related-party flows (regime break at the 2021-22
LODR tightening); business-group affiliation (value-additive historically per
Khanna-Palepu — a 25-year-old thesis to re-test); capital-cycle abuse (dilution + capex
+ debt growth as forward ROCE-degradation, the PSU/infra 2013-19 record). The
40-years-of-40%-ROCE compounder claim is probably survivorship until a
formation-decile test with a delisted registry says otherwise.

**The bridge to booked doctrine:** ROE-rank minus ROCE-rank divergence =
leverage-flattered economics = exactly the negative-convexity names SEC-D7 flagged
[BOOKED]. That divergence is the first fundamentals signal to test — it repackages a
doctrine we already trust.

**Forensics (sector-scoped):** cash-balance vs implied interest yield (the Satyam
tell); ALM tenor mismatch + related-party lending concentration (IL&FS/DHFL); loan
growth far above system + concentration (Yes Bank). The Ind-AS FY2016-17 seam is a
prerequisite gate for every fundamentals backtest — and it coincides with
demonetization/GST formalization shifts that must be disentangled first.

## E. The edge list (ranked, with status)

1. **Profitable-value combination** — the most replicated construction in the
   literature (GP × cheap, QMJ × value); the future stock-book's default sort. GATED:
   firm fundamentals (as-filed, PIT). [LIT, replicated]
2. **Cash-based operating profitability** as THE quality characteristic (one clean
   measure replacing GP + accruals). GATED: full statements. [LIT Ball et al.]
3. **ROE−ROCE divergence** as the leverage-negative-convexity screen — extends booked
   SEC-D7 machinery; bar = reproduce the cemetery signature OOS, sector-neutralized.
   GATED: fundamentals. [BOOKED bridge]
4. **Never unconditional size; small+quality only** [LIT AFIMP 2018] — binding
   constraint on any NIFTY-750 small/mid sleeve design.
5. **Promoter-pledge acceleration × falling price** as a distress overlay — free,
   genuinely PIT (SEBI quarterly), calibratable on real blowups; bar = survives
   excluding the famous four. GATED: SEBI patterns scrape. [LIT + case record]
6. **Quality regime-conditioning** — the 2010-20/2021-24 India whipsaw and the
   cheap-quality-predicts-QMJ fact both say: condition quality exposure on its own
   valuation spread, never hold it unconditionally. [LIT; test on live index segments]
7. **Avoid**: aggregate-payout timing [BOOKED QG-D1], chased growth/asset expansion
   [LIT], accruals-alone [superseded], Magic-Formula-as-published [failed replication],
   ratio screens across the Ind-AS seam, anything computed on restated/backfilled
   Indian histories without the PIT discipline.
