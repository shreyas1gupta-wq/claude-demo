The proposition

Charm gets the meeting. The product keeps the money.

₹25L

A promoter puts ₹25 lakh in as a trial, watches two quarters, then scales up or walks.

The second opinion
Would it help to have a completely independent read on what you already hold — no strings, just a second set of eyes?
The single view
Most families your size run money across five or six institutions. Would seeing all of it on a single screen be worth an hour?
Allocate
Rather than pitch a fund, let me show you how our model portfolio behaved through the last two corrections.

Three openings, none of which asks for a rupee.

05
The meetings

Earn the second, then the third

1

Understand

First meeting

Know two or three things moving in his sector — a policy shift, a deal, a margin trend. Open on his world. Offer to review what he has.

2

Show

Second meeting

Use his own numbers. Swap one fund he's unhappy with for your best. A small, winnable yes.

3

Raise it

By the third

Travel, sport, food. Be well-read in two of them. At this level that is how trust gets built.

One rule above all: never sell a product in the first meeting. And in discovery, ask “what is it about this that worries you most?” — not “how much?”
